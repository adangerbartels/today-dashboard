Slack

Checking…

Use a user token (xoxp- or xoxe.xoxp-) — only that knows what you've read. Scopes: channels:read, groups:read, channels:history, groups:history.

Token rotation

Tokens beginning xoxe.xoxp- expire after 12 hours. Fill these in and the app renews itself; leave them blank and you'll be pasting a new token twice a day. Client ID and secret are on your app's Basic Information page.

Important channels

Only checked channels are counted — that's the filter that keeps this lane focused.
